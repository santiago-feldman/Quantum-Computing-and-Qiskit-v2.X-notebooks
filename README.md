These four classes, along with their exercises, were developed for the Qiskit Fall Fest 2025, held at the Buenos Aires Institute of Technology (ITBA) in Buenos Aires, Argentina. The event was organized by the IEEE Computer Society student chapter. All content — classes and exercises alike — was designed and written by Santiago Feldman.
